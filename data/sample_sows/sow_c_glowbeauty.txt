GlowBeauty loyalty project - scope doc v3 FINAL (2)
===================================================

1. BACKGROUND
-------------

GlowBeauty is a cosmetics retailer with stores in Cairo, Alexandria and New Cairo. they want a loyalty program. This document captures the scope as discussed in the meetings of August 12 and August 19.

THIS DOCUMENT SUPERSEDES ALL PREVIOUS VERSIONS INCLUDING v2 AND THE DRAFT SHARED BY EMAIL ON AUGUST 14.

2. What we agreed
-----------------

Customers get points when they buy stuff. The earn rate is 5 points per 1 USD (see pricing annex — TBD).

GlowBeauty wants 12 offers for launch, possibly 15 if the marketing team approves the extra budget. For planning purposes assume 12.

Go-live is expected in Q1 2027.
